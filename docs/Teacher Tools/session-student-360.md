# Student 360

Open the **Student 360** tab and tap a student to see their profile: name, username, email, phone, date of birth, class, centre and student type.

<img src={require("./img/tt-student360.png").default} alt="Student 360" width="380" />

The **Temporary Password** row helps a student log in to the app in class. Do not share it with anyone else.
