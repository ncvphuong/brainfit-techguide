# Find and Print a CogMAP V2 Report
This feature is for SA, ML, MA, CA, Trainer

Use **Standard Score** to find any student's CogMAP V2 results, including students you don't have open.

1. Open **Reports** in the top menu and click **Standard Score** under **CogMaps V2**.

![Image](./img/cogmap-print-1.png)

2. Choose the centre (and class or round if you like), type the student's name and click **Search**. The table shows each question's score. Click the student's **name** to open their report.

![Image](./img/cogmap-print-2.png)

3. Choose the **Round** and the report **language**, click **Show**, then **Print**. In the print window, choose *Save as PDF* to keep a copy.

![Image](./img/cogmap-print-3.png)
