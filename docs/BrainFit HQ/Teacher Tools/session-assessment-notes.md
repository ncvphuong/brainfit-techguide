# Assessment and Notes

## Assessment

1. Open the **Assessment** tab and tap a student to expand them.
2. The student's assessment templates are listed. Tap one to fill in its criteria.

<img src={require("./img/tt-assessment.png").default} alt="Assessment" width="380" />

If it says **No assessment templates assigned**, the student has no templates yet. Templates are set up in the Assessment Tracker (see the Guideline section).

## Notes

1. Open the **Notes** tab and tap a student.
2. Earlier notes are listed. Type in **Write a note…** and tap **Add Note**.

<img src={require("./img/tt-notes.png").default} alt="Notes" width="380" />

Notes are the same notes as on the student's page in ACP.
