# Teacher Tools

**Teacher Tools** ([tt.brainfitstudio.com](https://tt.brainfitstudio.com)) is the phone and tablet app coaches use **during class**. Open today's session and, for each student in it, give points, enter Smart Moves and Junior scores, take attendance, write notes and look up the student.

It uses the same data as ACP: points, scores and attendance you save here show up in ACP straight away.

This feature is for Trainers, Assistant Coaches, Centre Admins, Master Admins and Master Licensees (and Super Admin).

The screenshots in these guides come from the practice training centre with pretend students.

## Sign in

1. Open [tt.brainfitstudio.com](https://tt.brainfitstudio.com) on your phone or tablet.
2. Sign in with your **ACP username and password**.

<img src={require("./img/tt-login.png").default} alt="Sign in" width="380" />

## Pick your centre

If you work at more than one centre, the centre name is shown at the top. Tap it and choose the centre you are teaching at. Everything you see (sessions, classes, students) is for that centre only.

<img src={require("./img/tt-centre.png").default} alt="Centre picker" width="380" />

:::tip
Add the page to your phone's home screen (Share → **Add to Home Screen**) so it opens like an app.
:::
